📌 Merk-Folie: Comparative & Superlative

Comparative – zwei vergleichen

Adjektiv + -er / more + than
The coat is warmer than the shirt.

Superlative – das Beste von allen

the + Adjektiv + -est / the most
The jacket is the coolest.

Achtung Plural!

trousers, shorts, shoes, boots, socks … → are
My trousers are dirtier than my shorts.
